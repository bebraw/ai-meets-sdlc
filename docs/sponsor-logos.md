# Sponsor logo sources

Downloaded from official sites on 2026-09-16. The `logo` field is the
light-background version; optional `logoDark` is the dark-background version.
The homepage uses `logo` on a shared light surface in both site themes so every
sponsor gets consistent contrast. Presentation slides use `logo` on the fixed
background specified by `logoSurface`. Dark variants remain available for other
placements.

- Reaktor: black and white SVGs extracted from the data URLs in the official
  [navigation stylesheet](https://www.reaktor.com/hubfs/hub_generated/module_assets/1/196825287539/1789556795666/module_Navigation.min.css).
- Swarmia: official [black SVG](https://www.swarmia.com/_astro/swarmia-logo-full.gbpHmnv8.svg)
  and [white SVG](https://www.swarmia.com/_astro/swarmia-logo-full-white.WkOXRIq2.svg).
- Aalto University: English university logos from the official
  [logo generator](https://aaltologo.fi/), large, matching variant 1:
  [black PNG](https://aaltologo.fi/logos/logo-86626-1.png) and
  [white PNG](https://aaltologo.fi/logos/logo-86630-1.png).
- Wunderdog and Aimbition: black and white Sevendos Group PNGs supplied by
  the sponsor. Original artwork, group endorsements, and built-in spacing are
  retained. Homepage logo sizes step down from Epic to Tech to Brand, with an
  optical size adjustment for Wonna's stacked mark.

Wonna and Rakettitiede retain their existing assets and fixed logo backgrounds.

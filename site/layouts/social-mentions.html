About this collection

These are English paraphrases of selected LinkedIn and Bluesky posts, comments and follow-ups, with links to the originals. Finnish material is summarized in English. Some LinkedIn discussions may require sign-in; links to a parent post or containing thread are labeled accordingly.

The collection is a qualitative sample of visible reactions. Authors can appear more than once, and speaker, sponsor and institutional relationships are stated alongside their contributions. It is not a satisfaction survey or a count of independent endorsements. Reported experiments and discussions do not establish measured business impact.

Follow-up discussion and website feedback have their own categories; they do not establish seminar attendance or programme feedback.

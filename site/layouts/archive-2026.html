Venue

The seminar took place on the Saastamoinen Foundation Stage in Marsio at Aalto University, Otaniemi: a high-production setting for talks, demos, and direct discussion between academia and industry.

Address: Marsio, Otakaari 2A, 02150 Espoo

See the FAQ for public transport, parking, food, and afterparty information.

Organizers

SDLCAI is organized by Toska Osuuskunta, the cooperative behind Future Frontend.

In addition to this seminar and Future Frontend, the group has previously organized the React Finland and GraphQL Finland conferences.

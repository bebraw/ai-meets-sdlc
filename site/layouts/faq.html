How to get to Marsio, where to park, and what to expect once you arrive. Here are the practical details for your day at SDLCAI.

13 October 2026 / Marsio, Espoo

01 / Parking

Where can I park?

Paid visitor parking is available in outdoor parking spaces on Aalto University's Otaniemi campus and in the Otaparkki car park at Sähkömiehentie 3.

Parking payments are handled through EasyPark or ParkMan. Check the signs where you park for the applicable restrictions and payment details.

See Aalto's driving and parking guide for the parking map, current fees, and charging points for electric cars.

02 / Venue

Where is the seminar?

SDLCAI takes place on the Saastamoinen Foundation Stage in Marsio, on Aalto University's Otaniemi campus in Espoo.

03 / Getting here

How do I get to the venue?

We recommend arriving by metro (subway) or tram. Both bring you within a short walk of Marsio.

By metro: Get off at Aalto University (Aalto-yliopisto) station and follow the signs for exit A towards Otaniementie / University at the end of the platform. From there, walk across Korkeakouluaukio square and cross the light rail tracks. Marsio is the first building on your left after the tracks.

By tram: Take light rail 15 to the Aalto University (Aalto-yliopisto) stop. Marsio is a short walk from the stop, beside the light rail tracks.

Plan your connections with the HSL journey planner using Marsio as your destination. Aalto's arrival guide has more details for the final walk to the building.

04 / Afterparty

Where is the afterparty?

The afterparty is at the same venue, Marsio, after the seminar programme. Stay around to meet other attendees, continue the day's conversations, and unwind together. There is no need to travel to another location.

See the full schedule for the day's programme and afterparty timing.

05 / Food

Will there be food?

Yes! Your seminar ticket includes food and refreshments throughout the day:

  • Lunch during the seminar day.
  • Coffee and pastries during the breaks.
  • Food at the afterparty at Marsio.

The schedule shows when to pause for lunch, catch up over coffee, and join the afterparty.

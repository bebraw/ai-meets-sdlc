Research behind the cut

Give every image a reason

The new cut gives the talks more space, uses the live programme as its running order, and builds motion around the subject. These opening films and demoscene references informed the direction and the editing approach.

  • Web Unleashed 2019 / Jam3

    Build the visual language from the subject. Browser tabs, windows and scrollbars become the material of this developer-conference opener. For SDLCAI, branches and feedback loops can shape the film.

  • Semi Permanent 2018 / Joyce N. Ho & Worship

    Let typography carry some of the energy. Distinctive letterforms give the titles character; our names and talk titles then need a calm, readable hold within each introduction.

  • TEDxSydney 2019: Legacy / Substance

    Make scale mean something. A fictional orbital archive gives its grand architecture a clear function. The SDLC machine similarly needs recognizable stages and a change moving through them.

  • FITC 2020: What You May Find / Sarofsky

    Use the song to shape the story. The creators cut storyboard sketches to the lyrics before building their 3D scenes. This informed the earlier “A Foolish Game” prototypes; the full cut carries that attention to musical phrasing into “Cipher.”

  • Second Reality / Future Crew 1995 / Kewlers & mfx

    For the demoscene direction, let computed forms carry the identity. The Finnish demoscene references lead to original vector forms and flowing grids, with gentler motion while the audience reads each talk title.

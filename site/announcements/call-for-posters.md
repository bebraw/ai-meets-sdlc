**Update, 5 October 2026:** The call for posters has closed.

We opened a call for posters for SDLCAI 2026, with space for between five and ten posters during the seminar at Marsio, Aalto University, on 13 October.

The submission deadline was 27 September 2026. We reviewed proposals as they arrived and made acceptance decisions on a rolling basis.

## What we are looking for

We welcome applied and academic work about AI across the software development lifecycle. A proposal might present research findings, an industry case study, an experiment, a method, a tool, a failure, or work in progress.

The subject can touch any part of software delivery: planning, requirements, architecture, implementation, review, testing, deployment, operations, maintenance, governance, developer experience, or organizational change. We are interested in concrete work that gives attendees something useful to question, compare, or take back to their own teams.

Product and commercial experience can be relevant, but the poster must teach or investigate something. Sales pitches and advertisements will not be accepted.

## Format and practical details

Posters must be A0 or A1 in portrait orientation. Accepted presenters are responsible for printing and bringing their own poster, installing it at the venue, and removing it after the event.

The poster session runs from 12:30 to 13:30 on 13 October at Marsio. A designated presenter should attend the session to discuss the work with seminar participants. Note that the posters can be available for the whole day and you will have good chances to interact with the attendees even during the breaks.

Each accepted poster includes one SDLCAI event ticket for its designated presenter.

## How selection works

We selected proposals for their relevance to SDLCAI, specificity, learning value, and contribution to a balanced mix of academic and industry perspectives. Acceptance was rolling: proposals were reviewed as they arrived, and a poster space could be offered before the submission deadline.
